Hello! I am Frank (Fu) Chen, a first-year M.S. student in Computational Data Science (MCDS) at School of Computer Science, Carnegie Mellon University (CMU SCS). I received my B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence Stream) from CUHK-Shenzhen in July 2026. 

**I am considering an AI4Bio PhD beginning in Fall 2028 or an MLE/applied-scientist role in industry in 2027.**

My interests include machine learning, AI for biology, and reliable agent systems. In my undergraduate years, I committed to research in machine learning and AI for biology. I started my research journey with building tools for genetic data analysis on batch effects and cell distribution topology, supervised by Prof. Tianwei Yu and Prof. Jingyi Jessica Li respectively. I also worked on integrating LLM tools with general medical knowledge and data on Chinese Medicine and using traditional algorithms to analyze phylogenetic trees. Through these experiences, I developed a strong foundation in machine learning, data analysis, and computational biology, which I am eager to further explore and apply in my graduate studies and future career.

In industry, I have worked through internships at Shenzhen Siku (AI start-up), Microsoft, and Alibaba Inc. I built and optimized agent systems for end-to-end programming and code review at Microsoft, and for capturing and labeling item-level e-commerce foundational properties at Alibaba.

Outside work, I grew up in Fujian and enjoy watching sports games, simulating public transit (or building virtual cities in *Cities: Skylines* if you happen to know it).
